## Scope

This section covers the public per-team mini-game catalog and the play routes that exist behind it.

The published contract is `GET /api/games/catalog`. Play routes for daily spin, guess-the-player, live games, and pick-em are implemented and consumed by native, but they are not all in the generated OpenAPI yet.
