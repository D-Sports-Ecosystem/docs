## Main routes

- `GET /v1/engage/rep/breakdown`
- `GET /v1/engage/rep/ledger`
- `POST /v1/engage/rep/earn`

All three require Clerk bearer auth.

## Runtime behavior highlights

- Breakdown returns `totalFanRep`, `fanAmpRep`, `clubRep`, `playerAmpRep`, `dsportsDirectRep` (always `0`), and `clubRepByTeam`.
- Ledger uses keyset pagination on `(createdAt DESC, id DESC)`.
- Ledger `source` is `fanAmp` | `club` | `playerAmp`. `dsportsDirect` never appears.
- Earn accepts only `reaction`, `message`, `pollVote`, and `share`.
- Arena check-in and daily-quest completion write Rep on their own routes. Do not also call `/earn` for those events.

## Common outputs and side effects

- New `RepLedgerEntry` rows for accepted earns
- Updated breakdown totals on the next read
- No leaderboard or `PointsHistory` writes from the generic earn route

## Known caveats

- `dsportsDirectRep` stays `0` until a producer exists.
- Club totals can be larger than the sum of `clubRepByTeam` when some Club `targetId`s are not teams.

## Exclusions

- Admin grant/override paths, if any, are not public documentation.
