## Feature parity with backend/PWA

`d-sports-engage-native` (1.25.9) targets high parity with `d-sports-api` feature behavior while optimizing for mobile UX patterns.

## Wallet and pack opening

- Wallet screens consume balances, token data, and PIN-gated custodial flows from backend APIs.
- Sends use server-side `sign-transaction`, not on-device private-key signing.
- Pack browsing, purchase, and open flows use backend-driven states and inventory refresh behavior. Start a pack purchase with `POST /api/packs/{id}/purchase`. Do not call `PUT` on that path (`410 GONE`). Confirm crypto checkout with `POST /api/checkout/crypto/verify`.
- Odds-disclosure surfaces read backend-provided probability payloads.

## Native review requests

Shop and wallet pack reveals call `lib/store-review.ts` after a Rare, Epic, Legendary, or Mythic pull. The helper uses `expo-store-review` on iOS and Android and skips web or unavailable APIs. With MMKV storage available, requests are limited to once per installed app version and at least 90 days apart. The OS decides whether to display the review sheet; a request does not guarantee presentation. Game-win and quest-claim moment names exist in the helper type but are not wired triggers. This flow is separate from the app-update prompt.

## Binder and market

- `app/binder` is the digital binder inventory and transfer surface. It talks to `/api/binders*`.
- Binder PIN is not the wallet PIN. Native must store the short-lived verify-pin token and send `x-binder-pin-token` on binder reads.
- You browse packs on `market/packs` and trading on `market/trading` so one tab does not lose the other. Listings go through `/api/v1/marketplace/*` (in generated OpenAPI). `listings/mine` still works; `me/listings` is the live path.
- Card history uses public `GET /api/v1/cards/{id}/provenance`.

## Quest, Fan Rep, rewards, and pass surfaces

- Quest UI supports team-aware progression and pass-gated eligibility.
- Daily quiz sessions use `/api/v1/engage/quests/*`.
- Fan Rep screens read `/api/v1/engage/rep/breakdown` and `/api/v1/engage/rep/ledger`, including `dsportsDirect` ledger rows.
- Reward states and claim flows follow backend quest/reward APIs.
- Global pass and event pass effects are reflected in native quest visibility/completion UX.

## Games

- The games surface joins `GET /api/games/catalog` with `GET /api/v1/engage/games/catalog`. See [Game Engine](/repositories/game-engine/index).
- Legacy keys (daily spin, guess-the-player) still drop when unknown. Engine keys stay visible as **Update required** or **Not in this build**.
- Shipped engine games on `/(tabs)/locker-room/games/{key}`: `daily-pickem`, `daily-trivia`, `prize-wheel-spin`, `guess-the-player`, `daily-lineup`, `card-trivia`, `login-streak-scratcher`, `deflection`, `iron-trap`, `deke-daily`, `faceoff-frenzy`, `sauce-pass`, `empty-net`, `air-hockey`, `locker-singularity`.
- `locker-singularity` uses the idle ledger (`/api/v1/engage/games/locker-singularity/ledger`), not session/action/complete. Send `X-Client-Version` and a client-held UUID `idempotencyKey` on mutations. The client sends tap counts, never buzz amounts.
- How-to-Play walkthroughs stay local. They must not call session/action/complete.
- Game Center uses `/api/events/*` for hero, lineup, player-stats, top-performer, win-probability, chat, presence, ratings, Broadcast discovery, and Live Activity. Ratings submit through `POST /api/events/{gameId}/ratings`.

## Team and leaderboard integration

- Teams and leagues metadata are consumed from backend `/api/teams` and `/api/leagues`.
- Sports catalog comes from `GET /api/config/sports`. Team search uses `GET /api/teams/search`.
- Joining a team (`POST /api/teams/{id}/join`) is leaderboard membership. Following a team is a separate social action. Starring an HQ club (`PUT /api/me/starred-teams/{teamId}`) is a bookmark, capped at 50, and is neither a join nor a follow.
- Fetch one team with public `GET /api/teams/{id}` (id first, then slug). Archived teams return `404`.
- Leaderboard displays and team-scoped ranking views align with backend leaderboard contracts.
- Favorite-team interactions influence quest and leaderboard context where backend eligibility requires it.

## Native media, widgets, and calendar

- **Locker-room PiP:** the full-screen media viewer and inline video tiles opt in with `allowsPictureInPicture`. Inline tiles preserve active PiP playback when they leave the viewport or Reduce Motion is enabled. This is not an app-wide PiP guarantee for every video surface.
- **Rank and Fan Rep widgets:** `widgets/ios/RankOrPointsWidget.tsx` supports small/medium Home Screen layouts and circular, rectangular, and inline Lock Screen accessories. The small Home Screen layout prefers a valid team rank. Taps use an `engagenative://` deep link when supplied, otherwise the leaderboard for a valid rank or the wallet as fallback.
- **Live status widget:** `widgets/ios/LiveStatusActivityWidget.tsx` opens the match for a supplied `gameId`, otherwise the locker room. Its small layout keeps the LIVE label on one line. WidgetKit home/Lock Screen layouts are separate from ActivityKit Live Activities and Android widgets; keep platform-specific imports.
- **Add to calendar:** upcoming HQ cards and pre-game match actions open the system event editor on iOS/Android, not web. `lib/hq/add-to-calendar.ts` builds an event from the fixture start time with a 150-minute duration, optional venue, match deep link in notes, and a 30-minute pre-game alarm. Invalid dates or unavailable APIs do not create an event. The helper distinguishes saved, opened, canceled, and error outcomes: Android's `done` response means the editor was opened, not confirmed saving. There is no silent calendar write or server-side calendar synchronization.

See [native configuration](/repositories/d-sports-engage-native/architecture#native-configuration-and-ios-capabilities) for plugins, privacy declarations, and build requirements.

## Tier 2 iOS behavior

- **App Shortcuts:** Open Packs opens the wallet; Scan Arena QR opens the leaderboard scanner; Live Score opens a validated explicit match ID or the tracked live match, falling back to `/hq`. The shipped Swift Live Score shortcut supplies no ID. These actions open the app and navigate; they do not open packs, submit check-ins, or speak a fetched score. Pending invocations wait for the in-app auth/onboarding gate; a burst navigates to the newest recognized target, and unknown intent names have no target.
- **Live Game accessory:** a tracked live snapshot shows a score/status bar above native tabs, or a compact inline score beside minimized tabs. Tapping opens the match. While mounted, it fetches the tracked match through existing `GET /api/events/{id}` immediately, every 30 seconds while active, and on foreground. Network failures keep the last score; final, canceled, or postponed games clear the accessory while preserving the MMKV snapshot for ActivityKit cleanup. It does not discover live matches.
- **Preview and share:** market `ListingCard` and `TradeListingCard`, plus the team browse card's details link, use `Link.Preview` and open/share menus for native iOS interactions. Sharing strips Expo route groups and defaults to `https://app.d-sports.org`; the team share action requires a string href. This is not an app-wide preview or external metadata service.
- **Sheets and resizing:** HQ game summaries and the legacy wheel/player-guess routes use native iOS form-sheet detents. Match routes and engine game routes are not converted wholesale. Large iOS media-viewer windows remain free of programmatic orientation locking under the 600-dp shortest-side heuristic; compact iOS and Android keep existing viewer behavior.

## Spotlight search and Handoff

On iOS, the authenticated in-app bootstrap indexes teams from `user.favoriteTeams`, active-wallet binder summaries, and owned collectibles using existing data sources. Binder mutations and foregrounding refresh binder summaries; wallet changes clear stale binder rows. Posts and marketplace listings are not part of the Core Spotlight batch.

Team search results open `/hq/{slug}`; binder results open `/binder/{id}`; card results open `/binder`, not a selected card. Continuations wait for the in-app gate. The parser accepts Engage identifiers, internal paths, and HTTPS URLs on the configured or canonical app host, rejecting other schemes/hosts. Route guards and backend authorization remain authoritative.

Focused HQ clubs, non-global team boards, binder/card detail, visible or owned market listings, and ready post detail publish Handoff activities. The activity clears on blur. Sign-out, settled signed-out/session-revoked startup, and account switching invoke index and activity cleanup, including cancellation of pending index updates. Native failures are logged; cleanup is not a guarantee of synchronous OS deletion. Handoff offers continuation, not cross-device state synchronization or a new search API.

See [Tier 2 configuration](/repositories/d-sports-engage-native/architecture#tier-2-native-integration) and [Spotlight integration](/repositories/d-sports-engage-native/architecture#core-spotlight-and-handoff-integration) for source ownership and native-build requirements.

## Notifications and legal

- Push delivery uses OneSignal. In-app inbox uses `/api/notifications*` and includes `GAME_ALERT` and `RATING`.
- Settings persist per-channel notify flags on `PATCH /api/user/settings`.
- About can check for an EAS OTA update without waiting for the next cold start.
- Locker-room posting gates on the in-app EULA. Policy pages render in-app; do not hardcode legal copy in clients.
- Accessibility settings include high contrast and color-blindness palettes. The public support matrix is `GET /api/accessibility`.

## Admin hub

- `app/(admin)` exposes team-manager surfaces: packs, collectibles, coins, games, live, moderation, social, submissions, team access, users, and CMS.
- The client gate is UX only. Every `/api/admin/*` route re-checks RBAC server-side.
